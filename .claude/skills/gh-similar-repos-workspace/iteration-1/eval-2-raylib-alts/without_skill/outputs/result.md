## Notes

- **Closest match in spirit**: sokol (C headers, minimal, no magic) and SFML (C++, batteries-included, same audience)
- **Easiest onramp**: love2d (Lua scripting) or olcPixelGameEngine (single header, zero setup)
- **Most production-proven**: SDL — it's what most other frameworks wrap
- **Most raylib-like codebase philosophy**: cute_framework (one author, opinionated, games first)
